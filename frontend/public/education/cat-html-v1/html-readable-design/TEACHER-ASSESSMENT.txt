REASONING GUIDE
Does colourful text automatically improve a webpage?
No. Colour must preserve readability and serve a purpose. Very low contrast can make information difficult to read.

Why is Read the workshop plan more informative than Click here?
It tells the reader what to expect from the destination rather than describing a generic action.

Is font color modern HTML styling?
The example demonstrates a legacy presentational attribute still encountered in classroom material. Modern standards-based websites normally use CSS. The teacher should confirm current assessment syntax.
