REASONING GUIDE
Is alt a tooltip that always appears on the picture?
No. It is a text alternative, including for assistive technology and unavailable images; title is a separate attribute with different behaviour.

Why can club.svg load but Club.svg fail?
Paths can be case-sensitive on the hosting filesystem. Verify exact case, extension and folder. Local tolerance does not guarantee hosted success.

Does clicking a link upload the target file?
No. The browser follows its URL. The target must already exist at the resolved location.
