AUTOPROMOTE EDUCATION — CAT TEACHER REVIEW
Public professional teacher-review presentation. Original supplementary material; no CAPS approval, endorsement or measured learning-speed claim.
The founder reports interest from a practising South African CAT teacher; professional assessment has not yet occurred. No teacher identity or learner data is collected here.

Reference: DBE amended CAT CAPS Section 3 (2024), published on the DBE website. PDF pages 19–20: Grade 10 HTML; 26 and 29: Grade 11 tables, lists, images, attributes, links and troubleshooting; page 2 overview: Grade 12 table formatting and good webpage design. Teacher to confirm current annual teaching plan and assessment tag sheet.

This is six focused mini-lessons, not complete coverage of all CAT practical work. Broader Grade 12 table attributes and multi-page design remain an extension. Some legacy presentational tags/attributes remain in CAPS; the materials explicitly distinguish those from modern standards-based HTML/CSS.
Lessons 1–3 introduce structure, images/links and lists/tables. Lessons 4–6 extend this with heading hierarchy/presentational formatting, readable webpage design and an evidence-led broken-link/correction/retest. The .htm extension is not inherently invalid; references must match an existing file. The troubleshooting film uses a real local HTTP404; file:// sessions may show a different missing-file message.

All examples are fictional club content, run locally, with original vector artwork. No third-party music, learner accounts, production jobs or publication is involved.

Separate assessment: HTML correctness (12) and creative explanation/reproduction (8), total 20 per lesson. Suggested scores are teacher-review drafts, not official assessment marks.

Teacher review form:
1. Is the concept technically correct and appropriate for the intended grade?
2. Can a learner predict and explain a different browser result?
3. Are code, output and focus regions readable without guessing?
4. Can a beginner follow the shown clicks, scrolling and parameter values?
5. Does motion clarify the relationship rather than distract?
6. Is pacing suitable, with enough time to pause or predict?
7. What should change before a supervised classroom trial?
Record comments against lesson ID and video time. Do not send learner names or records. Teacher feedback/independent learner recreation remain pending.
