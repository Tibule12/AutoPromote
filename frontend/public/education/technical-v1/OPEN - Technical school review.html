Mechanical Technology · Grade 10 systems and control; Grades 11–12 extension

Two gears. One clear ratio.

Two external spur gears have equal module and pressure angle, with 20 driver teeth and 40 driven teeth. Pitch radii are 72 and 144 drawing pixels, fixed centre distance 216. For ideal rolling at the pitch circles, n₁z₁=n₂z₂ and θ₂=−θ₁/2 plus the initial tooth phase. The larger gear turns in the opposite direction at half the speed. A reduction transmits power and ideally increases torque; losses reduce output power.

Questions and reasoning guide

If the driver turns clockwise, what does the driven gear do?

Counterclockwise, because these are two external gears in direct mesh.

What is the output speed for a 120 rpm input?

60 rpm, since 120 × 20 / 40 = 60.

Does twice the ideal torque create extra power?

No. Ideal power is torque × angular speed: doubling torque while halving speed preserves power. Friction reduces actual output.

For a 30-tooth driven gear at drawing module 7.2, what changes?

Output is 80 rpm for 120 rpm input, ratio 1.5:1, opposite direction. Pitch radii 72 and 108 give centre distance 180 drawing pixels. New geometry and initial mesh phase are required.

Electrical Technology · Grade 10 basic principles; Grades 11–12 revision

Close the path. Light the load.

A low-voltage DC source supplies potential difference; conductors, a switch and a suitable lamp form a series path. Opening the switch breaks the path and the lamp is off. Closing it completes the path and permits current. Conventional current is shown through the external circuit from positive to negative; this is distinct from electron drift in metal. Moving indicators communicate direction, not measured current or the speed of electrons.

Questions and reasoning guide

Why is the lamp off with the switch open?

The conducting loop is interrupted. In this elementary ideal DC illustration, no steady current flows through the lamp.

Which way is conventional current shown?

From the positive terminal through the external circuit and load to the negative terminal. Electron drift in metal is opposite.

Do the moving dots measure current or electron speed?

No. Their speed and spacing are visual teaching choices. A simulation or measurement would require a circuit model and component values.

Questions and reasoning guide

What is a bed joint?

The horizontal mortar joint between courses. A perp joint is vertical between neighbouring bricks.

How far is the pattern offset?

Half the brick-plus-joint module: 80 drawing pixels for this 160-pixel module. These are illustration units, not a site dimension specification.

Are the cropped end fragments instructions for half-brick cuts?

No. They show a wall continuing beyond the displayed crop. End/corner details and actual cutting/bonding require the teacher’s technical drawing and supervised instruction.
