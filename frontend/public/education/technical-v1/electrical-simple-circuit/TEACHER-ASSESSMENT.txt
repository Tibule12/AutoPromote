SUPPLEMENTARY PRIVATE TEACHER REVIEW — NOT CAPS APPROVED
Close the path. Light the load.
Grade 10 basic principles; Grades 11–12 revision

OBJECTIVES
Identify source, conductor, switch and load in a simple DC schematic.
Predict lamp state for open and closed paths.
Trace conventional current through the external circuit from positive to negative.
Distinguish an illustrative animation from an electrical calculation or simulation.
Coordinate switch rotation, lamp opacity and current-marker position keys.

DRAFT RUBRIC /20
Correct component identification and open/closed reasoning (6).
Correct conventional current direction and distinction from electrons (6).
Clear simulation/supervision limits (2).
Switch/light/marker timings remain causally consistent (3).
Editable project, exact native controls and justified visual choices (3).

REASONING GUIDE
Why is the lamp off with the switch open?
The conducting loop is interrupted. In this elementary ideal DC illustration, no steady current flows through the lamp.

Which way is conventional current shown?
From the positive terminal through the external circuit and load to the negative terminal. Electron drift in metal is opposite.

Do the moving dots measure current or electron speed?
No. Their speed and spacing are visual teaching choices. A simulation or measurement would require a circuit model and component values.

LIMITS
Illustrative low-voltage DC schematic, not an electrical solver or practical wiring plan. 3V is an example source label; component ratings/current are not calculated. No mains activity or unsupervised workshop task. Any real practical must use teacher-approved components and direct supervision.
