SUPPLEMENTARY PRIVATE TEACHER REVIEW — NOT CAPS APPROVED
Build the bond, course by course.
Grade 10 Construction specialisation; Grades 11–12 revision

OBJECTIVES
Identify courses, horizontal bed joints and vertical perp joints.
Explain the half-module stagger in stretcher bond.
Sequence the courses from the prepared support upward.
Distinguish a cropped elevation from an end/corner construction detail.
Use native layers, group masking and eased vertical/opacity keys to reveal a sequence.

DRAFT RUBRIC /20
Correctly label courses, bed joints and perp joints (6).
Predict alternating half-module stagger and explain its purpose (6).
State crop/base/DPC and practical limits (2).
Correct native layer order, mask and course timings (3).
Recreate an editable sequence and justify the motion/annotation choices (3).

REASONING GUIDE
What is a bed joint?
The horizontal mortar joint between courses. A perp joint is vertical between neighbouring bricks.

How far is the pattern offset?
Half the brick-plus-joint module: 80 drawing pixels for this 160-pixel module. These are illustration units, not a site dimension specification.

Are the cropped end fragments instructions for half-brick cuts?
No. They show a wall continuing beyond the displayed crop. End/corner details and actual cutting/bonding require the teacher’s technical drawing and supervised instruction.

LIMITS
Supplementary front-elevation illustration, not structural design, a construction method statement or an alternative to supervised workshop training. No real dimensions, mortar mix, load capacity, foundation specification or DPC installation detail is supplied. The colour strip only identifies a contextual DPC layer. Cropped fragments continue outside the frame.
