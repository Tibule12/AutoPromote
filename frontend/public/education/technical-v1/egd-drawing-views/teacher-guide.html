Review purpose

Technical understanding and creative communication are assessed separately. Teacher feedback, independent learner recreation and actual school hardware trials are pending. No formal CAPS approval is claimed.

Learning objectives

  • Match visible edges of a stepped isometric object to its front, top and right-side views.
  • Distinguish pictorial appearance from dimension-preserving orthographic views.
  • Explain and use the stated third-angle layout.
  • Animate native outlines and matching colours to communicate corresponding views.

Suggested rubric /20

  • Correct front, top and right-side geometry and matching dimensions (6).
  • State third-angle convention and align the views correctly (4).
  • Explain a new dimension change without relying on colour alone (4).
  • Accurate native outline timing, readable labels and safe placement (3).
  • Editable Studio reconstruction and justified technique choices (3).

Reasoning guide

Where does the top view go in this stated convention?

Above the front view. This lesson explicitly uses third-angle projection.

Why is there a line at x=30 in the top view?

It marks the visible change from the tall portion to the lower step; it is not a separate slot.

What width does the right-side view show?

The depth, 50 model units; its height is 40. The front view width is 80.
