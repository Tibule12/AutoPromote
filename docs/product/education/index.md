## AutoPromote Education · IT concepts and creative editing

ID: `education.it_foundation` • **PARTIAL** • DETERMINISTIC

An IT-first learning section pairs the five byte-conversion foundations with four native motion concept labs: variable/loop state, conditional validation/debugging, bubble sort and database relationships. Every lesson teaches the concept and the actual Studio techniques.

**Purpose:** Help IT students understand and explain computational ideas through creative visual construction, prediction and transfer exercises, with teacher review before learner release.

**Available to:** Public initiative information after an approved release; Private local curriculum review; school enrolment is closed

**Media:** Original program screen recordings, Written steps, narrated workflow videos and editable Studio practice projects, Original empty stages expanded into editable native motion graphics, Concept explanations, exact layer/key tables and teacher reasoning guides

**Requires:** studio.timeline (PARTIAL), studio.export (PARTIAL)

**Optional:** studio.titles (FUNCTIONAL), studio.audio (FUNCTIONAL)

**Workflow**

1. Read the initiative and teacher information. School registration and minor onboarding are not open.
2. Released lessons must include actual Studio source, controls, moving result, narration and reviewed reproduction evidence. Unreviewed lessons remain hidden from the normal library.
3. In an existing authorised Studio account, explicitly load a curated practice recording through the normal upload flow and open a fresh editable project.
4. Predict a trace or query result before editing. Review the actual code or SQL, conceptual explanation, editable project, complete movie and a different unseen input. Device-local checklists are self-reported practice only.

**Preview:** The lesson site plays recorded teaching media. Practice opens the existing Studio; it does not simulate the editor or automatically start a render.

**Final render:** Practice exports use the normal owned Studio renderer and account cost controls. The initiative grants no unlimited processing or school entitlement.

**Limits**

- All nine lessons remain private review candidates, not teacher-approved or independently learner-reproduced releases. Per-artifact export and moving-preview evidence is required; passing technical checks alone does not qualify teaching quality.
- School-managed accounts, teacher and learner roles, safeguarded participation, consent arrangements, private school reporting and finite funded allowances are planned and not enabled.
- No compulsory purchases or targeted learner advertising are intended for a school programme. Do not onboard minors before the necessary school permissions and safeguards exist.
- English is the initial draft language. isiXhosa versions need language review before release. No faster-learning, accreditation, CAPS approval or education-authority endorsement is claimed.
- Production deployment and paid processing require separate owner approval. Existing normal account permissions and costs remain authoritative.

**Common errors**

- A lesson is unavailable: It may be unreleased or awaiting review. Open the learning library and do not infer availability from a private preview.
- Practice cannot open in Studio: Use an existing authorised account, upload the supplied recording through the normal controls, and check its source readiness and entitlement.

**Controls:** No source-qualified editable control is recorded.

**Guides:** No task guide in V1.

**Templates:** No pinned shared-template binding recorded.
