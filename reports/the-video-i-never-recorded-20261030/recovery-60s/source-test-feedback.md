## Feedback

- **Improvement:** the presenter is frontal and appears to address the lens in the sampled open-eye frames. The same broad face, hair, charcoal shirt, acoustic wall and microphone persist across the two takes.
- **Narration:** local speech recognition recovers both requested lines in full. Take 1 finishes its words around 6.30s; take 2 around 6.54s, leaving natural source handles.
- **Visible continuity concern:** take 2 has noticeably harder edge contrast/skin texture. Incidental microphone-arm lettering remains despite the no-logo prompt. Facial expressions also become more emphatic than the restrained direction at moments.
- **Not approved yet:** a consistent voice, believable acting and perceptual lip sync. Speech recognition verifies words, not voice identity or mouth synchronization. I have not claimed a listening verdict from measurements or still images. Use the joined file to judge the sound and mouth movement at the transition.
- **Playback:** every encoded source picture decodes; neither take contains black frames. Normal-speed headless local playback reaches the end of both. That observation dropped six/seven frames during concurrent playback and does not qualify smooth perceptual 24fps presentation; it does not by itself prove dropped encoded source frames. Both originals retain all 192 encoded pictures.

**Verdict:** improved frontal framing and useful visual continuity evidence, but insufficient evidence to approve full production. The sharpening difference and unqualified voice/lip-sync need review. No clock graphics or AutoPromote effects are present in these raw clips.
