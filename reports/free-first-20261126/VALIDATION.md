`browser-recordings/` contains actual headless, muted authoring/timeline recordings, including early failed observers. `finished-playback/` contains the actual completed movie playback. Early failed build/observer/test logs are retained. The upload probe failure was a genuine emulator transport issue exposed when the old billing-pilot flag was disabled; the existing production offset-recovery behavior is retained. The export admission failure exposed the auth middleware's normalized `claims.email_verified` shape and is corrected with a spoof-profile regression case.

The successful real workflow is deliberately narrow: a one-second, one-source title edit. It proves normal account access, authoring, save/reopen, a real native output and accounting. It does not qualify long-form throughput, all effect combinations, background/Blender/GPU operations, every production dispatcher, host loss, every storage/download path, subscription settlement against live PayPal or external publication to six providers. Those require the migration gates and, for paid tests or deployment, explicit approval.

Current measured invoice-attributed operation prices are unavailable. Fresh aggregate telemetry and configured resource shapes are real observations; per-operation prices, merchant-specific fees and the scenario calculator remain estimates. There is no claim that the proposed price/allowances have passed financial validation.
