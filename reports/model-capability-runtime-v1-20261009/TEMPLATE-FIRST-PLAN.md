## Concrete first output

A 20-second product-launch promo using real moving source footage, deliberately animated typography, a restrained brand reveal, a call to action and timed music/SFX. Proposed edit: 0–2s hook; 2–8s product in use; 8–15s two feature/payoff beats; 15–20s brand reveal/CTA. These timings are a starting specification, not a completed creative result.

Replaceable inputs: logo, brand colours, headline, two feature lines, CTA, three footage slots, music choice and aspect ratio. Preserve the same authored layout, movement and editorial grammar after replacement. Test long copy, wide/tall logos and different source framing; preserve legibility rather than silently shrinking text to fit. Use rights-cleared materials and do not present fictitious brands as actual customers.

## Execution order and gates

1. Establish one precise animation/style benchmark from an actual moving reference. The retrieved Motion Array listings establish customisation fields; they do not establish that their preview videos were watched. Specify the hook, rhythm, typography, logo treatment, camera/crop and sound before expensive rendering.
2. Author short CPU/local previews of the headline, product treatment and logo/CTA. Inspect actual motion and listen to the soundtrack. If the visual direction is weak, revise the design rather than adding neural jobs. Technical generation cannot substitute for professional art direction.
3. Implement slot binding and versioned instantiation into the existing Studio document. Use real footage to complete the full 20-second edit. Fix only the shared compiler/renderer gaps exercised by this template.
4. Exercise the normal authenticated Studio path: apply, change slots/timing, undo/redo, save, close/reopen, preview, export and download. Compare mapped frames/cuts/text/layout/audio with the preview and inspect the full rendered movie. A private signed-out fixture or successful command is insufficient.
5. Repeat the same template on three rights-cleared test brands with different assets; inspect all three outputs. Record input/template/project/output hashes, cost, accepted creative scope and exact defects. Three manually hardcoded films do not prove template replacement.
6. After full lifecycle and exact-byte creative approval, derive the public before/after/poster/final copies from this same template execution. Connect the welcome page to the gated manifest and pin the template identity through its existing authentication flow. Verify WATCH EXAMPLE and MAKE ONE LIKE THIS separately. A future hero needs all additional hero gates.

The first creative gate is one convincing short animation sample. The first integrated deliverable is one playable promo plus its editable native project. The reusable-template gate is three distinct branded outputs through the same normal Studio path. Broader catalogue work follows only after those gates pass. No completion date or reference-level quality is claimed by this proposal.
