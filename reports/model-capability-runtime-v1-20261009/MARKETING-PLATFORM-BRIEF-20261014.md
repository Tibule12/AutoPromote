Prepared before the new packaging edit, 14 October 2026. Audience: independent video podcasters and the editors who repurpose their recordings. Problem: publishing an episode does not create a readable, well-framed short; starting the social edit again adds work. Outcome to demonstrate: turn an existing spoken moment into a portrait clip with editable emphasis, framing, music and sound. This proof preserves a manually chosen moment. It does not claim automatic highlight selection, transcription, new multicamera direction or a measured time saving.
