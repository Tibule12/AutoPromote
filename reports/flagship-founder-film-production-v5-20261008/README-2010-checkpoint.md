## What blocks the film

The existing VACE 1.3B control path cannot currently deliver a photographic, identity-preserving paper-to-yacht morph across this scale change. Edge control allows two vessels; full-RGB control keeps one vessel but imprints the crude guide and replaces the final frame abruptly. Another seed with the same adapter is unlikely to address the cause. The next capability is a geometry-anchored, region-aware transition: track one hull and sail, preserve their object identities, change paper→timber and paper→canvas on those regions, match camera/water depth, then refine/render in a way that retains both the native beginning and photographic yacht continuity. Review the whole moving shot with sound before accepting it.

For the human beat, generate actual moving action with the rope visibly linked to the boom and maintain the same navy/cream outfit across the deck close-up and sailor portrait. A still with a video container is not a movie shot. The retained 14B I2V provider took about 17 minutes for a prior shot; it could not safely fit in the remaining paid window. Use the existing private Content Factory asset provenance and Studio/Director assembly contracts once footage passes review. The older 51-second hero and its derivatives remain rejected private prototypes.

The new ident template is [original-ident-v6.py](gpu-package/original-ident-v6.py) with [editable request](gpu-package/original-ident-v6-preview.json). The render contains 120 NVIDIA OptiX frames, an editable `.blend`, timed audio cues and receipts. Its material/camera motion is a basis for a stronger integrated intro, not a release-ready Netflix-style studio identity.
