# AutoPromote flagship film: The Small Boat

**Private creative reset, 8 October 2026.** The podcast, quota, interview, and UI montage edit is rejected. This treatment supersedes that sequence. ## The story

An ordinary folded paper boat sits on a worktable. A drop of water spreads across the paper. The table seems to become an ocean; the little boat is suddenly too fragile for the scale of its world. Its creases become structure, the folded sail becomes canvas, the hull becomes timber, and the camera carries us into a real yacht moving through the same wave. One sailor aboard the real vessel hauls a mainsheet before the wave arrives, then takes the helm and commits to the open water. The opening identity is the ignition of that world, not an unrelated bumper.

Why should a stranger care? They understand a tiny vessel confronted by an overwhelming sea, and they want to see whether it survives. The paper-to-yacht change is the first answer; the sailor choosing to act is the emotional payoff. The ordinary starting material and impossible transformation imply what made the film without explaining a tool list.
