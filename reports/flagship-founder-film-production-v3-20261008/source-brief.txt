I want you to create the flagship AutoPromote welcome-page film.

This is NOT a generic SaaS promo.
This is NOT a feature montage.

I want AutoPromote to tell the story of how AutoPromote itself was built.

CORE IDEA
────────
The viewer should watch a cinematic development story:

one problem
→ more problems
→ repeated failures
→ obsession
→ breakthroughs
→ AutoPromote becoming bigger than the original idea
→ AI Director / generation / GPU capability
→ final reveal:

“The film you just watched was created by AutoPromote.”

That final reveal is important.

The advertisement itself must become proof of the product.

STORY DIRECTION
────────
The film should feel personal, cinematic and real.

Start small.

AutoPromote originally came from trying to solve painful creator/media workflow problems.

Show the progression:

- multi-camera footage
- syncing
- audio drift
- automatic camera switching
- rendering problems
- captions
- social clips
- reframing
- creator workflow
- VFX
- AI Director
- generated content
- GPU problems
- failed quota attempts
- infrastructure work
- debugging
- finally getting serious GPU execution
- AutoPromote beginning to create, understand and edit content itself

Do NOT turn this into a boring chronological documentary.

Find the emotional story inside the development journey.

There should be:
- curiosity
- frustration
- escalation
- setbacks
- breakthrough
- transformation
- payoff

HOOK
====
The first few seconds must be strong enough that a stranger stays.

Do not begin with:
“AutoPromote is an AI platform…”

Find a cinematic hook.

Possible direction:

“I was trying to solve one editing problem.
I didn’t know I was building this.”

Then immediately show something visually impressive from the finished system.

STORY PRINCIPLE
────────
The viewer must always understand:

What is happening?
Why does it matter?
What changed?
What are we waiting to see next?

No random montage.
No disconnected generated clips.
No meaningless VFX.

Every shot must advance the story.

CREATIVE STANDARD
────────
I want “how the hell did they make that?” moments.

Use the strongest capabilities we have where they improve the story:

- generated raw footage
- cinematic AI imagery/video
- Blender / 3D
- motion graphics

- then transition into the breakthrough
- “0” transforms into “3000 milliGPU”
- environment changes
- RTX PRO 6000 reveal lands with proper payoff

That is the level of storytelling I mean.

SELF-REVIEW
────────
After every rough render, review it like a viewer.

Ask:

- Does this make sense?
- Is there a real story?
- Is the hook strong?
- Does anything feel boring?
- Are there confusing sections?
- Is the payoff worth waiting for?
- Are effects helping or distracting?
- Does the final reveal land?
- Would someone ask how this was made?

If not:
fix it and rerender.

Do not accept “technically successful.”
